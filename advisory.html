A second opinion on the decision that shapes everything after it.

One-to-one sessions for founders deciding whether, when, and how much capital to raise, grounded in your own numbers instead of a template.

What we work through

The capital decision is rarely one question. A session pulls it apart so you can see which part is actually hard.

  • Raise or bootstrapThe full cost of each path: dilution, your time, and delay, side by side.
  • TimingWhat changes if you raise in six months instead of now, or twelve.
  • SizeHow much capital this stage really needs, and what it should buy.
  • The whyControl, signal, and optionality: what you are trading, and what you are keeping.

How a session works

  1. Bring your numbers

    Run the Capital Ledger beforehand if you like. The result is a useful starting point, and the Ledger can copy your numbers for the booking notes.

  2. Talk it through for 30 minutes

    We test the assumptions behind your numbers, look at what the math does not price, and name the questions still open.

  3. Decide what comes next

    Some founders leave with their answer. Others want deeper work, which can be arranged after the session.

Who this is for

Early-stage founders, from pre-seed to seed, who are weighing outside capital against other ways to fund the next stage.
