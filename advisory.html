Three decisions we work through

The same habit runs through all three: ask when and why before how. Each connects to one of the courses, and you do not need to have taken it.

Raise

Whether and when to raise

The Price of Money

  • The full cost of raising and of not raising: dilution, your time, and delay, side by side.
  • What changes if you raise in six months instead of now.
  • How much capital this stage really needs, and what it should buy.
  • Control, signal, and optionality: what you are trading, and what you are keeping.

Build

What to build toward

Value Before Valuation

  • Which parts of the business are creating value, and which only feel like progress.
  • What the next investor or an eventual buyer will pay for, and what they will discount.
  • The decisions in front of you that compound: hiring, pricing, equity, runway.

Exit

Whether and when to sell

The Last Term Sheet

  • Whether selling, holding, or waiting fits what you want from the company.
  • How to read an offer on more than its headline price.
  • What to prepare now so you have options later.
Education and consulting, not deal work.Sessions on selling a company help you think the decision through. Fitzroy & Co. does not find or introduce buyers or sellers, negotiate or arrange transactions, or receive fees tied to a sale.

How a session works

  1. Bring your numbers

    Run the Capital Ledger beforehand if the decision is about raising. The result is a useful starting point, and the Ledger can copy your numbers for the booking notes.

  2. Talk it through for 30 minutes

    We test the assumptions behind your numbers, look at what the math does not price, and name the questions still open.

  3. Decide what comes next

    Some founders leave with their answer. Others want deeper work, which can be arranged after the session.

Who this is for

Early-stage founders, from pre-seed onward, who are weighing a major capital, growth, or exit decision.

Session details and rates are shared when you book.
