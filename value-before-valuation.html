Build value before anyone prices it.

Value Before Valuation is a course on what creates company value, and which founder decisions build it or erode it, long before an investor or a buyer puts a number on the company.

The questions it answers

The first cohort is still being designed. These are the questions it is built around.

  • What actually creates value in a young company?And what only looks like value from the inside.
  • What do investors and buyers pay for?And what do they quietly discount?
  • Which decisions build value or erode it?Hiring, pricing, equity splits, runway, and the choices that compound.
  • How do you show progress before there is a valuation?The evidence that makes the next round, or the eventual sale, an easier conversation.

Who it is for

  • Founders from pre-seed to early growthWho want to know what to build toward, not only how to raise.
  • Founders who have raised and want to compound itUsing the capital to build value the next investor or buyer will pay for.
  • Teams that advise foundersAccelerators, programs, and mentors who coach value creation.
