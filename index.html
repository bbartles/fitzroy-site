Three costs, one column

Most founders weigh a raise against nothing. The Capital Ledger puts the real costs of a funding decision side by side, in dollars.

Dilution

Equity is a claim on your future. At a $10 million exit, 15% is $1.5 million, whether or not you ever felt it leave.

+

Your time

A raise takes months of founder hours. Those hours have a price, and so does every other way to fund the same stage.

+

Delay

Moving slowly is not free, and neither is moving fast on someone else's terms. Delay belongs in the same column.

Added up, that is the price of the money.

A teacher's framework, built on deal experience

Brian Bartles teaches venture finance and entrepreneurship at SMU's Cox School of Business. His background spans investment banking, venture capital, private equity, and hedge fund management, and he has founded and run companies of his own.

The Price of Money grew out of one observation: capital is the most expensive thing most startups buy, and the one they price least carefully.
