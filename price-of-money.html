The when and why of raising capital

Most fundraising instruction covers the what and the how: the deck, the process, the term sheet. It rarely asks whether raising is the right move, or whether now is the right time.

The Price of Money asks both. It puts dilution, founder time, and delay in one currency, so a funding decision can be argued with numbers instead of instinct. Then it makes room for what numbers cannot capture: control, signal, and the freedom to choose later.

Who it is for

  • Pre-seed foundersDeciding whether, when, and how much to raise.
  • Founders with another way to fund itWho could reach the next stage on revenue, consulting, or savings instead.
  • Teams that advise foundersAccelerators, programs, and mentors who coach this decision.

Questions

Is the Capital Ledger financial advice?

No. It is an educational tool that shows how the costs of a funding decision add up under the assumptions you enter. For advice on your specific situation, talk to a qualified lawyer, accountant, or financial professional.

What happens to the numbers I enter?

They stay in your browser. Nothing is sent or saved unless you choose to email or copy them yourself.

Why does the Ledger count my time?

Because a raise is not free even when the money is. Months of founder hours go into it. The Ledger gives those hours the same price as your best alternative use of them, so both paths are measured the same way.
